USA Wrestling cards

A wrestler cannot book a membership, walk-in, Friday Night Live, camp, event or lesson unless their card is current on every date booked. Missing cards block the same as expired ones.

How checking works

When a family adds or changes a card number, the site checks it on USA Wrestling’s public verify page and marks it verified if the athlete membership is active and the name matches. Anything else waits for you: check it, then mark it verified or not valid. A review stops applying when the family changes the number or date.
