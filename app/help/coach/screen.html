Today's roster and check-in

Open Roster & check-in. You see today's practices for the programs you coach and every extra practice, such as Friday Night Live, with every wrestler who is paid, on scholarship or booked to pay at the academy. Tap Check in, or Undo if you made a mistake.

  • The roster opens on the session that is running or next; the tabs under the title switch sessions. Scan a pass (at the bottom on a phone, Scan at the top) points the camera at a family's QR pass. Each wrestler is checked in once, into the session closest to now. A second scan just says they're already in.
  • A red-outlined row with Hold is a wrestler whose USA Wrestling card is missing, expired, not valid or not yet verified: they can't be checked in until the owner sorts it out or allows an exception. A wrestler whose parent hasn't signed the waiver or the Parent Consent Form is held the same way, and no exception lets them in: ask the parent to sign in their family account. Tap Why? for the reason. A red Allergy flag opens the family's note and the numbers to call; for everyone else they are behind Contact and notes.
  • Pay at the academy: a wrestler whose family booked a walk-in or an extra practice to pay cash shows “$40.00 cash due”. Take the cash and tap Cash received; it is recorded as paid and the roster shows “Received by” your name and the time. Your name is the one on your profile, or your name on Coaches. The family gets a receipt that says it was paid in cash at the academy. You can check them in before or after they pay. Hand the cash you collected to the owner at the end of the night: Payments shows what each coach collected.
  • Tapped Cash received by mistake? Only the owner can undo cash, so tell the owner.
  • The roster shows only your own programs’ practices and the extra practices, and never families' email addresses.

Your contract

When the academy publishes a coach contract, your coach home shows a to-do. Open Your contract, read it, type your name, draw your signature and sign. You can view or print your signed copy there any time. If the academy publishes a new version, you are asked to sign again.

Lesson openings and booked lessons

On your coach home, add one-hour openings and choose a lesson type for each:

  • Open time: a family books it as a one-on-one, or asks for it as a private partner or small group and invites other families. These show as family-requested.
  • One-on-one only: one wrestler.
  • Partner lesson (2 wrestlers) or small group (choose 3 to 6 spots): listed for every family as coach-hosted, one spot per wrestler, until it fills.

Each lesson shows its roster: every wrestler with their parent's name and phone, how many spots are left, and whether you posted it or a family asked for it. A wrestler marked Not paid yet has a spot kept by the family who organized the group. Each family pays the academy for their own wrestler at the academy's price per wrestler. You also get an email for each booked wrestler.

Repeat adds the same time every week, for up to 12 weeks, each with the same lesson type and spots. A week that overlaps one of your openings is skipped.

Who can book: a Public time shows for every family on Book a private lesson, and open ones on the public calendar. A Private time shows nowhere: only someone with its link can book it. Choose when you add a time, or later with Make private or Make public. Copy link for parents gives a link to text or email a parent; it opens that lesson to book after they sign in, for a Public or a Private time. A family’s own private group has its own link, which the family sends.

Use Change lesson type to change an opening you posted. Once a wrestler is booked the type stays, and the spots can't go below the wrestlers booked. You can't remove an opening after a family books it; ask the owner to move or refund it. Removing an open time a family asked for as a private group calls their request off, and they get a notice.

The owner can also add, change or remove your openings, with the same rules. They show on your coach home like the ones you add, families book them with you and you are paid for them as usual. You get an email when the owner adds or removes one.

Practice plans

Choose Practice plans on your coach home. The month calendar shows your programs’ practices and every extra practice, camp, clinic and event, with holidays and today marked. A practice marked Plan shared opens the plan the owner shares for it.

Your hours and time off

Open Your hours from your coach home.

  • Clock in when you start a practice and Clock out when you leave the mat. The same button is on your coach home.
  • Practices are paid for the scheduled practice time, by the role you coached. Time before or after the practice isn’t paid. The page shows the rate for each role.
  • Clocked in or out at the wrong time? Open the shift, choose Fix the times and say what happened. The academy sees the note.
  • Forgot to clock in? Add the shift by hand, for this month or last month, up to today. The academy sees that it was added by hand.
  • Clocked in by mistake? Open the shift and choose Remove this shift. It is deleted and isn’t paid. You can do this until you send its pay period. If the academy removes one of your shifts, you get an email saying which shift and why.
  • You are paid twice a month. On the last day of each pay period (the 15th, and the last day of the month), or after it, choose Send to send that pay period to the academy. Clock out first. If an earlier pay period still has shifts to send, Your hours says so with a button that opens it.
  • Private lessons are paid separately, from what families paid for them.
  • Time off: choose Ask for time off, pick the first and last day and give a reason. Only the academy sees your reason. If the academy approves, the other coaches in your programs are told you are away (not why), and the academy arranges cover. Nothing is canceled. You get a notice with the answer.

Your profile

Open Your profile (it is also under Account on your coach home) to set the name families see, a short bio and your specialties. Families see them beside your lesson times. Your phone number is only for the academy.
