Help for coaches

What you can do as a coach. Sign in with your email and your authenticator app.

  1. Today's roster and check-in
  2. Lesson openings and booked lessons
  3. Practice plans

Today's roster and check-in

Open Roster & check-in. You see today's practices for the programs you coach, with every wrestler who is paid or on scholarship. Tap Check in, or Undo if you made a mistake.

  • Scan passes: tap it and point the camera at a family's QR pass. Each wrestler is checked in once, into the session closest to now. A second scan just says they're already in.
  • A red edge means the wrestler's USA Wrestling card or waiver needs attention. Check-in still works; let the owner know.
  • The roster shows only your own programs and never families' email addresses.

Lesson openings and booked lessons

On your coach home, add one-hour openings. Families book them as private lessons at the academy's prices. A booked lesson shows the wrestler, the lesson type and how to reach the parent. Once the academy sets up email, you'll also get an email for each booked lesson. You can't remove an opening after a family books it; ask the owner to move or refund it.

Practice plans

Choose Open practice plans on your coach home to read the plans the owner shares for your programs.
