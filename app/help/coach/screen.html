Today's roster and check-in

Open Roster & check-in. You see today's practices for the programs you coach, with every wrestler who is paid or on scholarship. Tap Check in, or Undo if you made a mistake.

  • Scan passes: tap it and point the camera at a family's QR pass. Each wrestler is checked in once, into the session closest to now. A second scan just says they're already in.
  • A red edge means the wrestler's USA Wrestling card or waiver needs attention. Check-in still works; let the owner know.
  • The roster shows only your own programs and never families' email addresses.

Lesson openings and booked lessons

On your coach home, add one-hour openings and choose a lesson type for each:

  • Open time: a family books it as a one-on-one, or asks for it as a private partner or small group and invites other families. These show as family-requested.
  • One-on-one only: one wrestler.
  • Partner lesson (2 wrestlers) or small group (choose 3 to 6 spots): listed for every family as coach-hosted, one spot per wrestler, until it fills.

Each lesson shows its roster: every wrestler with their parent's name and phone, how many spots are left, and whether you posted it or a family asked for it. A wrestler marked Not paid yet has a spot kept by the family who organised the group. Each family pays the academy for their own wrestler at the academy's price per wrestler. Once the academy sets up email, you'll also get an email for each booked wrestler.

Repeat adds the same time every week, for up to 12 weeks, each with the same lesson type and spots. A week that overlaps one of your openings is skipped.

Use Change lesson type to change an opening you posted. Once a wrestler is booked the type stays, and the spots can't go below the wrestlers booked. You can't remove an opening after a family books it; ask the owner to move or refund it. Removing an open time a family asked for as a private group calls their request off, and they get a notice.

Practice plans

Choose Your month and practice plans on your coach home. The month calendar shows your programs’ practices and every extra practice, camp, clinic and event, with holidays and today marked. A practice marked Plan shared opens the plan the owner shares for it.

Your profile

Open Your profile to set the name families see, a short bio and your specialties. Families see them beside your lesson times. Your phone number is only for the academy.
