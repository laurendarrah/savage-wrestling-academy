Today's roster and check-in

Open Roster & check-in. You see today's practices for the programs you coach and every extra practice, such as Friday Night Live, with every wrestler who is paid, on scholarship or booked to pay at the academy. Tap Check in, or Undo if you made a mistake.

  • The roster opens on the session that is running or next; the tabs under the title switch sessions. Scan a pass (at the bottom on a phone, Scan at the top) points the camera at a family's QR pass. Each wrestler is checked in once, into the session closest to now. A second scan just says they're already in.
  • A red-outlined row with Hold is a wrestler whose USA Wrestling card is missing, expired, not valid or not yet verified: they can't be checked in until the owner sorts it out or allows an exception. Tap Why? for the reason. A red Allergy flag opens the family's note and the numbers to call; for everyone else they are behind Contact and notes.
  • Pay at the academy: a wrestler whose family booked a walk-in or an extra practice to pay cash shows “$40.00 cash due”. Take the cash and tap Cash received; it is recorded as paid and the roster shows “Received by” your name and the time. Your name is the one on your profile, or your name on Coaches. The family gets a receipt that says it was paid in cash at the academy. You can check them in before or after they pay. Hand the cash you collected to the owner at the end of the night: Payments shows what each coach collected.
  • Tapped Cash received by mistake? Only the owner can undo cash, so tell the owner.
  • The roster shows only your own programs’ practices and the extra practices, and never families' email addresses.

Your contract

When the academy publishes a coach contract, your coach home shows a to-do. Open Your contract, read it, type your name, draw your signature and sign. You can view or print your signed copy there any time. If the academy publishes a new version, you are asked to sign again.

Lesson openings and booked lessons

On your coach home, add one-hour openings and choose a lesson type for each:

  • Open time: a family books it as a one-on-one, or asks for it as a private partner or small group and invites other families. These show as family-requested.
  • One-on-one only: one wrestler.
  • Partner lesson (2 wrestlers) or small group (choose 3 to 6 spots): listed for every family as coach-hosted, one spot per wrestler, until it fills.

Each lesson shows its roster: every wrestler with their parent's name and phone, how many spots are left, and whether you posted it or a family asked for it. A wrestler marked Not paid yet has a spot kept by the family who organised the group. Each family pays the academy for their own wrestler at the academy's price per wrestler. Once the academy sets up email, you'll also get an email for each booked wrestler.

Repeat adds the same time every week, for up to 12 weeks, each with the same lesson type and spots. A week that overlaps one of your openings is skipped.

Use Change lesson type to change an opening you posted. Once a wrestler is booked the type stays, and the spots can't go below the wrestlers booked. You can't remove an opening after a family books it; ask the owner to move or refund it. Removing an open time a family asked for as a private group calls their request off, and they get a notice.

The owner can also add, change or remove your openings, with the same rules. They show on your coach home like the ones you add, families book them with you and you are paid for them as usual. You get an email when the owner adds or removes one.

Practice plans

Choose Your month and practice plans on your coach home. The month calendar shows your programs’ practices and every extra practice, camp, clinic and event, with holidays and today marked. A practice marked Plan shared opens the plan the owner shares for it.

Your profile

Open Your profile to set the name families see, a short bio and your specialties. Families see them beside your lesson times. Your phone number is only for the academy.
