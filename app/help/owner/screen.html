Every day

  • Until everything is set up, the dashboard starts with a Launch checklist: programs on the welcome page and calendar, practice schedule, waiver, coaches, lesson prices, payment setup, a recent backup and site health. Each item that still needs doing has an Open button that takes you there. It folds away once everything is ready.
  • On a new site, families see no programs until you choose Publish the programs on the checklist. It lists SWA Development, SWA Intermediate and SWA Advance with the prices under Programs & pricing. Then set the practice days and times under Schedules.
  • Everything in the workspace menu is real: it opens a real page or a setting families see. To check what families see, use Welcome page and Calendar at the top.
  • The dashboard shows today's sessions and check-ins, wrestlers blocked by a card or waiver, unread family messages, waitlists, money collected this month and site errors. Each tile opens its page.
  • Roster & check-in: check wrestlers in by hand or with Scan passes. Choose any date to see a past or future roster; print it if you want paper.
  • If Site errors shows a number, open Site health and share it with whoever maintains the site.

USA Wrestling cards

USA Wrestling cards lists every wrestler, problems first. Check a card with USA Wrestling, then mark it Verified or Not valid with a short reason the family will see. After a rejection, any change the family makes waits for you to check it again. Pick a future date (for example a tournament) to see whose card will have lapsed by then.

When a family adds or changes a card number, the site checks it on USA Wrestling’s public verify page by itself. If the athlete membership is active, the name matches and the family chose this season, the card is marked verified and the Status column says USA Wrestling lookup · Nov 6. Cards you verified say Owner. Anything else waits for you, with what USA Wrestling showed: not active, no card with this number, or the name on the card. Those cards also show under Blocked or expiring soon. If USA Wrestling can’t be reached, the card simply waits for you, as before. A not-active or mismatched card is not blocked automatically: mark it Not valid if it should be.

Check with USA Wrestling checks one card now. The answer is kept for a day, and only a few checks an hour are allowed. You can always override: mark the card Verified or Not valid yourself, and both are logged on Security activity.

Look up on USA Wrestling copies the card number and opens USA Wrestling’s membership check in a new tab. Paste it as the USAW ID, check the card is current, then come back and mark it. Every card expires on August 31, so in August the page counts the wrestlers who haven’t renewed for the new season yet.

Allowing booking anyway

For a specific situation (renewal still processing, paper proof at the door), open Allow booking anyway, choose an end date and a reason. The family can book sessions inside those dates. Remove it any time.

Waivers

On Waivers, paste the attorney-approved text and publish. From then on every wrestler needs a signature before booking, and families are told. Published text can't be edited: publish a corrected version instead, and families sign again. Stop requiring this waiver lifts the requirement at once. Exceptions work the same way as for cards.

Payments

  • Online payment opens per purchase type only after you decide its terms and refund policy. Until then families review online and pay you directly.
  • Record an offline payment on Payments: choose the family, wrestler and what they paid for, check the price, then enter the amount, method and date. It counts exactly like an online payment and the family gets a notice.
  • Void an offline payment entered by mistake. Online payments are refunded in Stripe and update here automatically.
  • Filter by date, type or method and Download CSV for your bookkeeping.
  • Income and expenses shows the academy's books by month and year. Card and offline payments, monthly payments and refunds appear on their own; add rent, equipment, insurance and other expenses, or other income such as a sponsorship, with a note and a receipt reference. Download a month or a year as a spreadsheet. Card payments are shown before Stripe fees.

Memberships, approvals, transfers and scholarships

  • SWA Advance approvals: approve or decline requests and reply in the private conversation. The family gets a notice.
  • Program transfers: move a paid membership to another program from a date. Grade and approval rules are checked; earlier rosters stay as they were. No money moves; record any price difference on Payments or refund it in Stripe.
  • Scholarships: create an offer, save the attorney-reviewed terms, and send the family the invitation link. They sign to accept.

Camps, events and waitlists

Create camps and events, with dates, prices and seat limits, under Camps & events in the workspace menu; they publish to the calendar. When an event is full, families can join its waitlist. On Waitlists, choose who gets a freed seat and how long it's held. Tick pass on to offer it automatically to the next family if the time runs out.

Coaches, lessons and coach pay

  • Coaches: invite a coach by name, email and programs. You get a link to copy and send; it works once, only for that email, for 7 days, and New link makes a fresh one. The coach creates their account, sets up an authenticator app and lands on coach home. Edit any coach's profile, change their programs, or remove their access: their past lessons and pay records stay. A removed coach can then be removed permanently.
  • You as a coach: on Coaches, add yourself as a coach to post lesson openings and take on programs from Coach view. You keep your owner account, and your lessons count in the coach pay report.
  • Private lessons: set the price per wrestler for each lesson type (one-on-one, partner, small group); blank keeps it off online booking. Every family pays the price for their own wrestler, so a partner lesson with two wrestlers is two payments. Set each coach's share of lesson pay, or a default.
  • Group lessons: a coach can post a partner or small group (3 to 6 spots) that every family sees, or a family can turn a coach's open time into a private group and invite other families by link. Every wrestler in every lesson needs a current USA Wrestling card and a signed waiver.
  • Recording a lesson payment: on Payments, choose the family and wrestler, choose Private lesson, then the time or group. Groups show their spots left, and private groups show which family organised them. A payment for a full lesson, or for a wrestler already in it, is refused.
  • Coach pay: lessons each coach taught in a period and what they're owed, with CSV export. A group lesson has one line per wrestler, and the coach's share is of everything paid for it.

Messages

On Messages, send an announcement to all families, one program's members or one event's registrants, and keep a private conversation with any family. Families also get automatic notices (card expiring, waiver to sign, waitlist seat, approval decision, payment recorded, lesson booked, transfer). Email delivery turns on once an email service is set up; until then families read everything in their account.

Public website

On Public website, write the welcome page's headline, introduction and mission, choose which coaches appear on the team page and in what order, and list wrestler achievements (name, achievement, season) and alumni. Coaches show the name, bio and specialties from their profile, never their email or phone. Wrestlers appear by name only, with no photos. Preview shows your changes first; Save and publish puts them live within a minute. An empty list is left off the page.

Backups

On Backups, download an encrypted backup at least weekly and before any big change. Keep the file and its passphrase somewhere safe outside the site. A backup includes families, payments, attendance, signed waivers and everything else; restoring only re-creates missing records and never changes existing ones.

Family data requests

Families can download their own data at any time. When a family asks to close their account, it appears on Data requests and on the dashboard. Contact them, do what your retention policy says (payment records and signed waivers may need to be kept), and record what you did. Nothing is deleted automatically.

Before each season

  1. Update practice schedules and prices in the workspace.
  2. Publish this season's waiver if it changed.
  3. Check USA Wrestling cards for the season's start date.
  4. Download a backup.
