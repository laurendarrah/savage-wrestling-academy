Get started

  1. Create an account with your email and confirm it from the email we send.
  2. Open your family account, add your name and phone, and add each wrestler with their grade. The Get set up list there checks off each step as you finish it.
  3. Add each wrestler's USA Wrestling card number and expiration date.
  4. If the academy asks for a waiver, sign it for each wrestler (see below).

For extra security, turn on an authenticator app under Account security, in the Account menu of your family account.

USA Wrestling cards

Every wrestler needs a USA Wrestling card that is current on the date of anything you book, including every day of a camp. Without one, booking is blocked. At the door, a wrestler can be checked in only once the academy has verified the card, so add it well before the first practice.

  • Get or renew a card at usawmembership.com, then enter the number and choose the card’s season in your family account.
  • Every USA Wrestling card expires on August 31, whatever month it was bought. New-season cards usually open in mid-August, so renew before August 31 and choose the new season. If your card shows a different date, choose “My card has a different expiration date” and enter it.
  • When you add or change a card number, the site checks the card number on USA Wrestling’s public membership check. If the card is active and the name matches your wrestler, you’ll see USA Wrestling card verified. Only the card number is sent to USA Wrestling.
  • If it says the card isn’t active, renew it at usawmembership.com, then choose Check my card. If USA Wrestling has no card with that number, or the name on it doesn’t match, check the number on the card: it may belong to someone else. A card is checked at most once a day, and only a few checks an hour are allowed.
  • If USA Wrestling can’t be reached, nothing changes: the academy checks your card by hand. If the academy can't verify a card, you'll see why; fix the number or date and the academy will check it again before booking reopens.
  • You'll get a notice 30 days before a card expires.

Waivers

When the academy asks for a waiver, each wrestler needs one signed before booking. Open Waiver, read it, type your name, sign with your finger or mouse, and confirm. You can view and print every signed copy. If the academy updates the waiver, you'll be asked to sign the new version.

Memberships, walk-ins and extra practices

  • 12-month membership: choose a program on Register. Programs match grades: SWA Development for K–6, SWA Intermediate for 7–12, and SWA Advance for 7–12 with coach approval.
  • SWA Advance: request approval on the Register page and talk it over with the coach in the private conversation there. You'll get a notice when it's decided.
  • Walk-in: pick one practice from the calendar on the Register page.
  • Friday Night Live and other extra practices are separate purchases, with a member price for members.
  • Pay cash at the academy: for a walk-in or an extra practice, choose Pay cash at the academy after you review it, then Book and pay cash at the academy. Your spot is booked straight away and your account shows “Pay at the academy: $40 due” (or that session's price). Bring the cash to the practice and hand it to the coach; once they record it, your purchase history shows “Paid in cash at the academy”. Changed your plans? Cancel it in your account, under Purchase history, any time before the practice starts. Nothing is charged.

Online payment opens one purchase type at a time. Until a type is open, a walk-in or extra practice can be paid in cash at the academy as above; for anything else, review your choice online and pay the academy directly, and it appears in your account once the academy records your payment.

Camps and clinics

Find camps on the calendar and book a seat for each wrestler. When every seat is taken, the camp is full and can't be booked. There is no waitlist.

Private lessons

On Book a private lesson, choose one of three ways to train:

  • Book a one-on-one: your wrestler alone with a coach. Choose your wrestler and one of the coaches' open times.
  • Join a partner or small group: a group lesson a coach has posted. Each one shows how many spots are left. Book a spot for your wrestler.
  • Start a private group: take an open time as a partner lesson (2 wrestlers) or a small group (3 to 6). Choose your own wrestlers, and you get an invite link to send to the families you want with you. Only families with the link can join, and the group is never listed for anyone else. Under Your private groups you can copy the link again, see who has joined and how many spots are left, and call the group off while nobody has paid.

Prices are per wrestler: every family pays for their own wrestler, and every wrestler needs a current USA Wrestling card and a signed waiver, just as for practices. If someone sends you an invite link, sign in, open the link, choose your wrestler and review.

Online payment for lessons isn't open yet. Pay the academy for your wrestler's spot; it's confirmed once the academy records your payment. You get a notice in Messages when the lesson is booked, and the coach sees your wrestler on their coach page.

At the door: entry pass and wrestler view

Each wrestler has an entry pass (a QR code) at Entry pass. Show it at the door and staff scan it to check your wrestler in. If the USA Wrestling card is missing, expired or not yet verified by the academy, your wrestler can't be checked in until it is sorted out. If a phone is lost or a screenshot was shared, choose Replace this pass; the old one stops working.

The wrestler view (link next to each wrestler in your family account) shows what's coming up, the pass and this month's check-ins. It's made for a phone.

Messages and notices

Open Messages in your family account for academy announcements, automatic notices (card expiring, lesson booked, payment recorded) and a private conversation with the academy. The newest notice opens by itself, and Mark all as read clears every new message at once.

Under Your data in your family account you can download everything the academy holds about your family, or ask to close your account.
