Get started

  1. Create an account with your email and confirm it from the email we send.
  2. Open your family account, add your name and phone, and add each wrestler with their grade. The Get set up list there checks off each step as you finish it.
  3. Add each wrestler's USA Wrestling card number and expiration date.
  4. If the academy asks for a waiver, sign it for each wrestler (see below).

For extra security, turn on an authenticator app under Account security, in the Account menu of your family account.

USA Wrestling cards

Every wrestler needs a USA Wrestling card that is current on the date of anything you book, including every day of a camp. Without one, booking is blocked.

  • Get or renew a card at usawmembership.com, then enter the number and expiration date in your family account.
  • The academy may check your card. If it can't be verified, you'll see why; fix the number or date and the academy will check it again before booking reopens.
  • You'll get a notice 30 days before a card expires.

Waivers

When the academy asks for a waiver, each wrestler needs one signed before booking. Open Waiver, read it, type your name, sign with your finger or mouse, and confirm. You can view and print every signed copy. If the academy updates the waiver, you'll be asked to sign the new version.

Memberships, walk-ins and extra practices

  • 12-month membership: choose a program on Register. Programs match grades: SWA Development for K–6, SWA Intermediate for 7–12, and SWA Advance for 7–12 with coach approval.
  • SWA Advance: request approval on the Register page and talk it over with the coach in the private conversation there. You'll get a notice when it's decided.
  • Walk-in: pick one practice from the calendar on the Register page.
  • Friday Night Live and other extra practices are separate purchases, with a member price for members.

Until online payment opens, you review your choice online and pay the academy directly. Your registration appears in your account once the academy records your payment.

Camps, clinics and waitlists

Find camps on the calendar. If a camp is full, choose Join the waitlist. If a seat opens, the academy can hold it for you for a set time; you'll get a notice and see Book now in your family account. Book before the time runs out or it goes to the next family.

Private lessons

On Book a private lesson, choose the lesson type, your wrestler and one of the coaches' open times. Partner and small-group lessons can list training partners. You get a notice in Messages when the lesson is booked, and the coach sees it on their coach page.

At the door: entry pass and wrestler view

Each wrestler has an entry pass (a QR code) at Entry pass. Show it at the door and staff scan it to check your wrestler in. If a phone is lost or a screenshot was shared, choose Replace this pass; the old one stops working.

The wrestler view (link next to each wrestler in your family account) shows what's coming up, the pass and this month's check-ins. It's made for a phone.

Messages and notices

Open Messages in your family account for academy announcements, automatic notices (card expiring, waitlist seat, lesson booked, payment recorded) and a private conversation with the academy. The newest notice opens by itself, and Mark all as read clears every new message at once.

Under Your data in your family account you can download everything the academy holds about your family, or ask to close your account.
