Encrypted backups & recovery

Backups include academy settings, saved family profiles, coach openings, scholarship offers and signed agreements, program approvals and their conversations, practice plans, curricula, and the security log. They do not include passwords, authenticator secrets, staff access grants, or the saved Stripe test key.

Create an encrypted backup

Use a unique passphrase with at least 12 characters and save it in your password manager. This passphrase stays in your browser and is never sent to the server. Keep the downloaded backup in a private, backed-up location. It cannot be opened without its passphrase.

Preview and restore a backup

Restoring replaces matching academy settings, family profiles, and coach openings. Scholarships, signed agreements, approvals, conversations, practice plans, and curricula are only added back when they are missing; an existing record is never changed. Records absent from the backup are kept. The security log, staff permissions, account passwords, and authenticators are never restored. A recovery snapshot is saved before any restore; the last 10 snapshots are retained. Backup files saved by earlier versions of this page still open here.

Recovery snapshots

Snapshots are created before restores, not automatically on a schedule. Download encrypted backups regularly; snapshots in this same database do not replace an off-site backup.

A snapshot is encrypted with the passphrase entered under Create an encrypted backup.
