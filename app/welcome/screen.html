How to join

  1. Create your accountSign up with your email address, then confirm it from the email we send you.
  2. Add your wrestlerEnter their grade and their USA Wrestling card number and expiration date.
  3. Sign the waiverWhen the academy asks for a waiver, sign it once for each wrestler, right on your phone.
  4. Choose trainingChoose a membership or a single walk-in practice, then pay to confirm your spot.

Every wrestler needs a current USA Wrestling card before they can book.
