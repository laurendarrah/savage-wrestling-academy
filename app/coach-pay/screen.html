Coach pay

What each coach has earned and what you have paid them. Record every payment you make to a coach here; it goes into Income & expenses as money out by itself, so don’t add it there as well. Earned is practice pay you have approved on Coach hours plus the coach’s share of private lessons already taught. Practice hours you have not approved are shown as not approved yet and are not counted as owed. Below that: paid private lessons by lesson date, with each coach's share from lesson settings. Each family pays for their own wrestler, so a partner or small-group lesson has one line per wrestler and the coach's share is of their total. Refunded and voided lessons are not counted. A lesson a family has paid for that is dated after today is marked Not taught yet; to pay only for lessons already taught, choose This month so far or Last 14 days.
