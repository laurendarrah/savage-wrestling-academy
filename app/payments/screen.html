Payment needs attention

Monthly memberships whose latest payment failed at Stripe. Stripe retries the card by itself and the family has been told. The wrestler stays on the roster for 7 days after the paid-through day; if Stripe gives up, the membership ends on the paid-through day. To settle it by hand, take a payment in Stripe or talk to the family.
