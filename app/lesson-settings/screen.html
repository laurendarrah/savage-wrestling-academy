Private lessons

Families book a coach's one-hour opening on the Book a private lesson page. Each price is per wrestler: every family pays it for their own wrestler, so a partner lesson with two wrestlers is two payments of the partner price. Leave a price blank to keep that lesson type off online booking. Online lesson payment stays off until the lesson refund policy is decided. Until then families pick a time and pay you directly, and you record the payment on Payments.
