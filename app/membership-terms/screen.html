Cancelling or leaving early

You can send a cancellation notice by email, phone, mail or in person, and also through your family account once online memberships open. You don’t need to give a reason. The academy will confirm that it got your notice, the date the cancellation takes effect, anything still owed, and any refund.

Leaving early (planned). Apart from the cancellation rights the law gives you, you can ask to be released early from a 12-month commitment. Monthly payments continue until a release takes effect under the final agreement. You can also ask the academy to consider medical or hardship relief.

These planned terms have no early-exit fee and no charge for the full unpaid balance. The final agreement will state the early-release and refund rules before you commit.

Your New York cancellation rights

Where New York’s Health Club Services Act applies, you can cancel without penalty within three business days after you receive the written contract. The law also lets you cancel in case of death, a disability lasting more than three months, a move more than 25 miles from any club the academy runs, or if the academy stops or substantially reduces the services in the contract.

Notices under these rights must be accepted within three business days. Any refund the law requires must be paid within ten business days, with deductions only where the law allows. You don’t need the academy’s approval to use these rights, and you can send your notice through the website or any of the ways listed above.

The final agreement will set out how these rights apply to SWA memberships.
