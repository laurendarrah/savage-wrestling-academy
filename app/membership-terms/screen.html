Cancellation and early exit

Cancellation notices can be submitted through the family account once live memberships are available, or by email, phone, mail, or in person. A cancellation notice does not require a reason. The academy will confirm receipt, the effective date, any remaining obligation, and any refund.

Proposed early-release rule. Outside a cancellation right required by law, a family may request early release from its 12-month commitment. Scheduled installments continue until a release takes effect under the final agreement. Requests for additional medical or hardship relief can be reviewed by the academy.

This draft does not add an early-exit fee or authorize an immediate charge for the full unpaid balance. Those charges have not been configured. The final agreement must state the approved early-release and refund rules before a family can commit.

New York cancellation protections

Where New York’s Health Club Services Act applies, the contract must preserve cancellation without penalty within three business days after receipt of the written contract. Additional cancellation rights cover death, qualifying disability lasting more than three months, relocation more than 25 miles from an academy-operated club, and discontinued or substantially reduced contracted services.

Applicable notices must be accepted within three business days. Required refunds must be issued within ten business days, with deductions only where the law permits. Statutory cancellation rights do not require discretionary academy approval. A family can use the website as well as the other accepted contact methods.

An attorney should confirm how these rules apply to SWA and complete the required contract language and any bonding requirements.
