Refunds for single purchases

These rules are final. They apply to walk-ins, Friday Night Live, camps, clinics, events and private lessons. Memberships follow their own terms below.

Walk-ins and Friday Night Live

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the practice starts for a full refund.
  • Later cancellations and no-shows are not refunded.
  • If the academy cancels the practice, for example for weather or a coach’s absence, you get a full refund.

Camps, clinics and events

  • Cancel at least 14 days before the first day for a full refund.
  • Cancel 7 to 13 days before the first day for a 50% refund.
  • Less than 7 days before the first day, there is no refund.
  • A single camp day follows the same rules, counted from that day.
  • If an injury or illness with a doctor’s note keeps a wrestler from attending, contact the academy. These are decided case by case.
  • If the academy cancels a camp, clinic or event, you get a full refund.

Private lessons

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the lesson for a full refund.
  • Later cancellations and no-shows are not refunded.
  • If the coach cancels, you choose a full refund or a new time.

How refunds are paid

  • Card payments are refunded to the same card within 10 business days of the cancellation.
  • To cancel, contact the academy using the details at the bottom of this page.
  • You can ask the academy for a written copy of this refund policy at any time.

Monthly membership

Automatic monthly renewal

  • The membership is month to month: no contract, no fixed term and no payment for the year upfront.
  • It renews automatically. Your card is charged your program’s monthly price, with any team code discount, when you sign up and again every month on the same day of the month, until you cancel. If a month doesn’t have that day, you’re charged on its last day.
  • Each payment covers practice through your next billing day.
  • Before you pay, the price, what the membership includes, and how to cancel are shown next to the box you tick to agree. You can’t pay without ticking it.
  • Right after you sign up, you get an email confirming these terms, the monthly amount, your next billing day and how to cancel. It is also saved in Messages in your family account.
  • Your monthly price stays the price you signed up at. Moving to another program doesn’t change it.

Canceling

  • You can cancel any time, for any reason, with no fee. You don’t need to give a reason.
  • Cancel online in your family account: choose Cancel membership on the membership, then confirm. You can also cancel by email, by phone or in person at the academy, using the details at the bottom of this page.
  • To stop the next charge, cancel before your next billing day.
  • When you cancel, no more payments are taken. Your wrestler keeps training through the end of the month you’ve already paid for, and the membership ends on your next billing day.
  • There is no partial refund for the rest of a month already paid for.

If a payment fails

  • You and the academy are told. Stripe, our payment processor, tries your card again over the following days.
  • Your wrestler can keep training for 7 days after the paid month ends while the card is retried. Once a payment goes through, the membership carries on as before.
  • If the payment still doesn’t go through, the membership pauses. Nothing more is charged, your wrestler comes off the practice roster after the last paid day, and you and the academy are told.
  • To restart a paused membership, choose Restart membership in your family account and pay with a card that works. Payments start again from that day, on that day of the month. Missed months are not charged.
