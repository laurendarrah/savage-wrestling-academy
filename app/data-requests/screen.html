Data requests

Families can download their own data from their account at any time. When a family asks to close their account, it appears here. Contact them, do what your policy says, and record what you did.

Nothing is deleted automatically. Payment records and signed waivers may need to be kept for tax, insurance or legal reasons; decide your retention policy with your attorney (see the policy drafts). To delete a family’s account, use Delete this family account on Family accounts: payments stay for the books without the family’s name, and signed waivers, scholarship records, check-ins and approvals are kept unless it is a test account.
