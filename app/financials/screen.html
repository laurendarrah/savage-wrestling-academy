How these numbers are counted

This is a simple record for running the academy, not an accounting system. Card payments are shown in full, and Stripe's card fees are an expense of their own (Card processing fees), read from Stripe once a day. A refund, full or partial, is money out on the day it was made, and a card payment a family disputed with their bank is money out on the day the bank took it (and back in if the academy wins). Payments instead takes a refund off the payment it belongs to, on the payment’s own date, so its totals can differ from these when a refund crosses a month. Record offline payments on Payments, not here, so they are not counted twice; pay owed to coaches is on Coach pay, and goes in here as an expense once it is paid.
