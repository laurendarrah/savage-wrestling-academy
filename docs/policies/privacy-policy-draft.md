This policy explains what information Savage Wrestling Academy ("the academy", "we") collects through our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. Our website is used by parents and guardians, adult wrestlers, our coaches and the academy owner.

## Who creates accounts

Accounts are created by a **parent or guardian** (or an adult wrestler). Children do not create their own accounts. A parent adds each child to the family account and can show the child a "wrestler view" and entry pass from the parent's own account.

## What we collect

**Information you give us**
- Parent or guardian: name, email address, phone number, and your password (held by our sign-in provider; we never see it).
- Your home address, and an emergency contact's name and phone number.
- Each wrestler: name, school grade, USA Wrestling card number and expiration date.
- Each wrestler's birth date, and any allergies or medical notes (or that there are none). This is health information about a child. The academy owner sees it, and coaches see allergies and the emergency contact on the check-in roster for their own programs. You can update it at any time.

- Signatures: when you sign a waiver or scholarship agreement, the drawing of your signature, your typed name, and the date and time.
- Messages you send the academy, and notes you add to requests (for example, an account closure request).
- Requests for SWA Advance approval, and the private conversation about them.

**Information created when you use the site**
- Registrations and purchases: what you bought, for which wrestler, the price, any discount, and the payment status. When you pay online, your card details go directly to Stripe, our payment processor. We do not receive or store your card number.
- Payments made in person (cash, check and similar) that the academy records for you.
- Attendance: the date and session each wrestler was checked in.
- Entry passes: a random code for each wrestler, shown as a QR code.
- Waitlist places and seat offers, private lesson bookings, and program transfers.
- The academy's review of a USA Wrestling card (for example, "verified" or "could not verify", with a reason).
- The result of checking a USA Wrestling card number: whether the membership is active, the name and state USA Wrestling shows for that number, and the date of the check. See "USA Wrestling card checks" below.
- Security records: sign-ins and sensitive actions (for example, a payment recorded or a waiver signed), with the time.
- Error records: when a page fails to load, the time and the page address (never the information you entered).

**Cookies**
We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in securely. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not sell or share your information for advertising.

## How we use it

- To run your registrations, memberships, camps, lessons and payments.
- To check wrestlers in at the door and keep rosters for coaches.
- To confirm that every wrestler has a current USA Wrestling card and a signed waiver before booking, as the academy requires.
- To reach someone quickly and look after a wrestler who is hurt or unwell at practice.
- To send you notices and messages (for example, a card about to expire, a waitlist seat held for you, a payment received) in your account and, [Decision: once email is set up], by email.
- To keep the site secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.
- To keep records the academy needs for accounting, insurance and legal reasons.

## USA Wrestling card checks

When you add or change a wrestler's USA Wrestling card number, or choose "Check my card", the site checks the card number with USA Wrestling's public membership verification page (usawmembership.com/verify_membership), the same page anyone can use without signing in. The academy may also check a card from its own page. Only the card number is sent to USA Wrestling: never your name, your wrestler's name, your email or anything else. We keep only whether the athlete membership is active, the name and state USA Wrestling shows for that number, and the date of the check. If the card is active and the name matches your wrestler, the card is marked verified. If the number belongs to someone else, we never show you their name. Each card number is checked at most once a day.

- **The academy owner** can see all family accounts and records.

- **Other families** never see your information.
