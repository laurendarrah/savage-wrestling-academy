# Refund and cancellation policy for single purchases (DRAFT)

Covers walk-ins, Friday Night Live and other extra practices, camps, clinics and events, and private lessons. **12-month memberships** are covered by the membership terms (#2), not here.

Online payment for each of these stays **closed** until its rules below are decided (#3, #80), because families must see the refund terms before paying. Once decided, they are shown on `/membership-terms` and on every review screen before payment.

## What the site already does

- Families see the price, dates and terms before paying, and must tick "I have read the purchase terms".
- Online refunds are made in Stripe. The site updates automatically: the purchase shows as refunded, and the wrestler comes off that roster.
- Payments made in person and recorded by the owner can be **voided** if entered by mistake. A cash refund is handled outside the site.
- A held waitlist seat can be released by the family ("Leave the waitlist") at no cost.
- If the academy cancels a session, the owner refunds in Stripe, or records the refund for an in-person payment.
